Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Clipboard is an iPhone app that saves what you copy and lets you paste it from a custom keyboard. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not, and the small amount of anonymous usage data it measures.

Your clips stay on your device

Every clip you save — text, images, categories, favorites, hidden items and the trash — is stored in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and never kept on a server. We cannot read your clips. Deleting the app deletes them.

The keyboard

The Clipboard keyboard asks for Full Access. iOS requires this before a custom keyboard can read data saved by its companion app — without it, the keyboard cannot show you your clips. The keyboard does not record, log or transmit anything you type. It contains no analytics and no network code of any kind.

Anonymous usage data

The main app (not the keyboard) uses two analytics services to understand how the app is used and to find crashes and problems:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — app launches, screen usage and standard device information such as device model, OS version and country.
  • PostHog — feature usage events, the language the app is displayed in, and counts (for example, how many clips or categories exist). Counts are numbers only; the content being counted never leaves the device.

This data is tied to a random identifier generated on installation. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and it cannot be traced back to you. Deleting and reinstalling the app creates a new identifier.

Session recording

PostHog session replay is enabled in the main app to help diagnose usability problems. Recordings are captured with all text inputs masked and all images masked, so the content of your clips is not visible in them. The keyboard extension is never recorded.

What we never collect

  • The contents of your clips, or anything you copy
  • Anything you type on the keyboard
  • Your contacts, photos, location or messages
  • Any account details — the app has no sign-in

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. The analytics services named above process data on our behalf as service providers, under their own privacy policies: Firebase and PostHog.

Children

Clipboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete any clip at any time from inside the app, empty the trash to remove clips for good, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. You can limit ad tracking and analytics identifiers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. To ask what data is held about your installation, or to have it deleted, email us at the address below.

Changes
