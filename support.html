Setting up the keyboard

  • Open iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and pick Clipboard.
  • Tap Clipboard in that list again and turn on Allow Full Access. Without it the keyboard cannot read your saved clips.
  • In any app, press and hold the globe key on the keyboard and choose Clipboard.

The keyboard is empty

Copy something, or open the app and add a clip with the + button. New clips appear in the keyboard straight away. If the list still looks empty, check that Full Access is on.

A clip disappeared

Deleted clips wait in Trash inside the app until you put them back or remove them for good. Clips can also be hidden — check the hidden list before assuming one is gone.

Deleting your data

Clips live only on your iPhone. Emptying the trash removes them for good, and deleting the app removes everything it stored. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
